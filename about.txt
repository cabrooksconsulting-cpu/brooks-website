About | Meet Carla Brooks
---
I am a CA licensed and nationally certified (ASHA) speech-language pathologist with a passion for helping people communicate more effectively. I have a wealth of experience across many different clinical settings in my 53 years of practice.  

For most of these years, my focus has been autism spectrum disorder and other developmental disorders.  More recently, I have introduced services for non-native English speakers who are seeking support in improving the clarity of their speech in their work settings as well as their community. 

My approach is warm, collaborative, and evidence based. I believe every person deserves to be heard and understood — and that the right support can open doors you never thought possible.

I provide services to clients of all ages and backgrounds, both in-person and virtually.
